### ▶ Horned Rabbit ◀

A twitchy highlands grazer mutated by Miasma bleed on the farms around **B. Hero's Point**. Small, fast, and mean when cornered. Highland elves joke that the horns are compensation; the kick afterward is not.

**Campaign canon:** Definitive trash mob for **Hero's Point** (Rank 0–1). Sylar's guide says "farm the monsters"; in this campaign, that means Horned Rabbits.

Trash tier: between **Cursed Head** and **Slime** in durability, without Pop! or Floating Head rules.

#### Abilities

**Standard Death:** Dies at 0 HP from any damage (chip damage counts). Suffers Critical Injuries normally. No Pop!, no crit-only kill rule.

**Skittish:** While Seriously Wounded, +2 to Evasion/Dance Base.

**Elite:** An Elite Horned Rabbit has 28 HP, Leather SP 6 (Head and Body), Horn Gore 4d6, and Hind Kick ROF 2.
