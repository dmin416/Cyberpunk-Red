# Elflines Online: Enemies

Combat reference for ELO: standard types, Elite blocks, campaign placement, variants, named bosses, homebrew roster, and loot tables. Sources: Interface RED Vol 1-2 (`Source/Full Text.md`). Skill Bases = STAT + Skill + modifiers already on the sheet.

**Homebrew** sections fill gaps Segotari never printed (Cinders roulette, Old Hero sub-bosses, Temple/Altar bosses, raid chest bundles). Marked explicitly.

**Hardened:** +2 to all attacking and defending Skill Bases ([Homebrew/GM Rules.md](../Homebrew/GM%20Rules.md)).

**Elite:** Apply the **Elite** line on each type below (HP, SP, damage, or special rules).

**Reskin rule:** Take any CP:R mook, swap highest ranged weapon for Melee Weapon or Archery, theme by cyberware/gear. Boosterganger = Gremlin, Security Officer with wolvers = Bearwolf. ELO fights end faster than street CPR; run more combats.

**Pop! (Slime):** On Critical Injury trigger, Slime dies immediately (no crit table). **Elite Slime** only dies via Pop! (chip to 0 HP is not enough).

## Standard types

### Bearwolf

In the high forests and mountains of the Elflands, the Bearwolf is the apex predator, defending its territory from both elf and monster alike with their razor sharp claws. Though fiercely protective of their cubs, some Gremlin tribes manage to steal them, raising the cub as personal mounts of the strongest Gremlins, or their spiritual leaders.

### Draglin

The results of The Warlock's attempt to create an army to rule The Elflines by infusing the Gremlins they captured with blood donated from the traitor dragon Miasmafire Chedul. **Base:** Gremlin (standard or Elite). **Where:** **G. Dripping Cove** (Sacred Herb farm PvP).

Same stat block as Gremlin. **Sacred Herb Sack:** on death, drops 1-3 **Sacred Herbs** (50% chance on pack clear). Cosmetic: hoe and herb basket.

**Loot:** Sacred Herbs (incidental), gp, Monster Part. See [G. Dripping Cove loot](#g-dripping-cove).
