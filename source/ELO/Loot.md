# Elflines Online: Loot Tables

Canonical drop tables for trash packs, dungeons, and raids. Item rules and vendor prices: [`Armory.md`](Armory.md). Boss blocks: [`Enemies.md`](Enemies.md). Published prose: [`Source/Full Text.md`](Source/Full%20Text.md).

**Book** = Interface RED / Daeric text. Pack roll procedure and named creature parts below are campaign rules. No other invented items.

---

## Book rule (trash)

> Most monster packs drop **gp**, a **monster part worth 50gp**, or **items from the Elflines Online Armory**. Segotari is stingy with powerful items; you have to kill **bosses** just to get a chance. **Excellent Quality** armory weapons are a good epic example; try not to give much of it. Dungeon **final bosses** pay gp ≈ street Job payout for that difficulty.

(IR1, Using ELO for GMs)

---

## How to roll a trash pack

Per cleared pack (or per solo kill if the pack is one mob): set **Overall Danger**, then roll **1d10**.

### Overall Danger

**Ranks 1–9:** Danger runs **5 → 50** (**Rank × 5**, except Rank **9 = 50**). **Raid trash = 100**. Rank 0 counts as Rank 1 → **5**. GM may raise it for Elite packs or PK pressure.

| Zone Rank | Overall Danger |
|-----------|----------------|
| 0–1 | 5 |
| 2 | 10 |
| 3 | 15 |
| 4 | 20 |
| 5 | 25 |
| 6 | 30 |
| 7 | 35 |
| 8 | 40 |
| 9 | 50 |
| Raid trash | 100 |

Final dungeon bosses: Job-tier payout (Book), not this table.

### Pack loot (1d10)

```
1     Nothing
2     gp = Danger × 1
3–5   gp = Danger × 2
6     gp = Danger × 3
7–9   Monster Item (creature table)
10    Armory Item (armory table)

Ex Rank 5 (Danger 25): 2→25gp · 3–5→50gp · 6→75gp
```

### Monster Item table

On pack loot **7–9**, roll **1d10** on the pack’s creature. Trade **50gp** each (book Monster Part). Do not reduce values.

```
Horned Rabbit     1–9 Horn · 10 Rabbit Foot
Gremlin           1 Loincloth · 2–10 Ear
Gremlin Gardener  1 Loincloth · 2–10 Sacred Herbs ×1
Any other         Monster Part

Specials (book, not this roll):
  Children of Slime (G) → Emerald Slimebow (GM %)
  K Elite Zazzolif key-carrier → Treasury Key (1/run)
  World Dragon → usually nothing useful
```

### Armory Drop table

On pack loot **10**, roll **1d10**. Vendor armory only (book prices).

```
1     1gp     Arrow ×1
2–3   10gp    Poison Arrow ×1 · or Arrow ×10
4–5   20gp    Leather Armor · or Arrow ×20
6–7   50gp    Studded Leather · Dagger · Shortsword · Sacred Herbs ×1
8–9   100gp   Chainmail · Longsword · Bow · Shield · Vial of Poison
10    500gp   Full Plate · Greataxe
```

## Raid notes (book)

### V. Twisted Eldertree
- **Scarlet Blackbow** (4d6, **ROF 2**; only bow type with ROF 2). Raid drop; multiple can exist (IR1: two {Fang_Hunters} PKers both used them on Daeric)
- Cinders bosses on the tree drop **nothing**

### W. Flooded Palace
- Bosses drop **nothing**
- All loot from the chest after Mother of Slime within the timer; fail = **zero**
- Chest contents not listed in book

### X. Pit of Dragons
- Loot saved until final round completes
- **Wolvar's Blessing**, **Dragonscale Armor**, **Dragonscale Helm** (Leather SP 10)

### Y. Warlock's Tower
- Bosses drop loot; specific items not listed
- Clear unlocks Heart of Miasma chamber access

### Z. Heart of Miasma
- **Heart of Miasma Pauldrons** (cosmetic) on full four-Elfline clear
- Chamber loot not listed
