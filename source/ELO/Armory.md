## Starting kit

New elves spend **200gp** from this list at creation. Keep leftover gp.

| Budget tip | Example loadouts |
|------------|------------------|
| Melee DPS | Studded Leather (50) + Longsword (100) + Shield (100) = 250 (need to trim or skip shield) |
| Bow | Leather (20) + Bow (100) + Arrow x20 (20) + Sacred Herbs x1 (50) = 190 |
| Tank | Chainmail (100) + Longsword (100) = 200 |
| Quickhand | Leather (20) + Dagger (50) + Shortsword (50) + Sacred Herbs x1 (50) + Arrow x30 (30) = 200 |

## Gear rules (all items)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Half SP** | Most ELO melee ignores half defender SP (round up). Bows use normal ablation unless item says otherwise. |
| **Repair** | **1 minute**; **half** CP:R gp cost ([`Mechanics.md`](Mechanics.md)) |
| **Excellent Quality** | CP:R Excellent Quality rules on armory weapons. Boss drops or **Weapon Polish** (TCG Joker 9). |
| **Concealable** | Dagger only (vendor melee). |
| **Loading** | Arrows load as part of the attack; no separate Reload Action. |
| **Shield** | 10 HP movable cover; equip/drop = Action; interpose vs seen attacks; cannot dodge that attack; destroyed at 0 HP until repaired. |
| **Bow range** | Bow/Crossbow range table (CP:R). Dragon breath uses Shotgun range. |

### BODY gates (Longsword & Greataxe)

| Weapon | Two hands | One hand | ROF 2 |
|--------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Longsword** | Required solo in ELO (below BODY 8) | BODY **8+** (CP:R) | Dual longswords **or** BODY **10+** |
| **Greataxe** | Required in ELO (default) | BODY **8+** or **Wolvar's Blessing** | BODY **10+** (homebrew) |

**Wolvar's Blessing:** Necklace from Pit of Dragons; Greataxe one-handed at any BODY.

## Ammunition & poison

| Item | gp | Use | On hit |
|------|-----|-----|--------|
| **Arrow** | 1 | Bow ammo | Normal bow damage; retrievable after firing |
| **Poison Arrow** | 10 | Bow ammo | **No bow damage.** Target: DV13 **Endurance/Resist Torture/Drugs** or **2d6** direct to HP. Armor not ablated. |
| **Vial of Poison** | 100 | Coat **Light Melee** (Action) | Lasts **30 min**. Hit: **no weapon damage**; DV13 or 2d6 direct HP. Armor not ablated. **Cannot coat arrows.** |

**Poison meta:** High-SP mobs (Zazzolif, plate tanks) often die faster to poison than raw DPS. **Slime Cloak** grants **immunity** to ELO poison (see Special gear).

**Poisonknife:** Dagger with permanent Vial-of-Poison coating. **K. Firsthold**, Treasure Golem (~5-10%).
