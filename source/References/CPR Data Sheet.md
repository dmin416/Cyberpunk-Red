# CPR Data Sheet

## Skill Resolution

Attacker's STAT + Skill + 1d10 vs. Defender's STAT + Skill + 1d10 or Task Difficulty Value (DV).

## Difficulty Values (DV)

| Difficulty | Description | DV |
|------------|-------------|-----|
| Simple | Something most people can do without thinking. | 9 |
| Everyday | Most people can do this without special training. | 13 |
| Difficult | Difficult without training or natural talent. | 15 |
| Professional | Takes training; skilled professional. | 17 |
| Heroic | Only the best of the best. | 21 |
| Incredible | Truly Olympian mettle. | 24 |
| Legendary | Awe-inspiring; spoken of for years. | 29 |

## Critical Success

Natural 10: roll another 1d10 and add. Do not roll again if you roll another 10.

## Critical Failure

Natural 1: roll another 1d10 and subtract. Do not roll again if you roll another 1.

## Trying Again

If you fail a Skill Check, you can't try again unless your chances of success have improved.

## Complementary Skills

With GM approval, a good complementary Skill Check grants +1 to the main Skill Check (once; do not stack).

## Taking Extra Time

+1 to a Skill Check by spending 4x the required time.

## Using LUCK

Each point of LUCK spent gives +1 to a Skill Check.

## Facedowns

COOL + Reputation + 1d10 vs. COOL + Reputation + 1d10

(If Reputation is for cowardice, treat it as a negative number.)

Loser: back down, or take -2 to future Actions against the winner until they defeat them once.

## Initiative

REF + 1d10

Your Turn = 1 Move Action + 1 other Action

## Move Actions

Move up to MOVE × 2 m/yds (or squares equal to MOVE).

Split Movement: take your Action in the middle of your Move, then keep Moving.

Misc. Movement: Swimming, Climbing, and Jumping with a running start cost 2 m/yds for every 1 m/yd traveled (or 2 squares per 1 square). Jumping from standing: half that distance.

## Ranged Combat Resolution

Attacker's REF + Relevant Weapon Skill + 1d10 vs. DV by Range and Weapon, or Defender's DEX + Evasion Skill + 1d10*

*A Defender with REF 8 or higher can choose to dodge instead of using the range DV.

## Fire Modes

**Suppressive Fire:** Action + 10 bullets. Everyone on foot within 25 m/yds, out of cover, in line of sight: WILL + Concentration + 1d10 vs. REF + Autofire + 1d10. Fail: must use next Move Action (and Run if needed) to get into cover.

**Autofire:** Action + 10 bullets. Use Autofire Skill and Autofire DV table. Cannot Aimed Shot. REF 8+ can dodge. Damage: 2d6 × amount you beat the DV (max = weapon Autofire: 3 SMG / 4 Assault Rifle).

## Aimed Shots

Entire Action, -8 to Check, max 1 ROF. On hit: damage + effect.

| Aiming for... | Effect |
|---------------|--------|
| Head | ×2 damage that gets through head armor. |
| Held Item | If any damage through body armor: drop one held item. |
| Leg | If any damage through body armor: Broken Leg (if any unbroken legs left). |

## Melee Combat Resolution

Attacker's DEX + Relevant Melee Attack Skill + 1d10 vs. Defender's DEX + Evasion Skill + 1d10

Melee Weapons and Martial Arts ignore 1/2 armor SP. Brawling does not.

## Brawling and Martial Arts Damage

Brawling: ROF 2. Cyberarm: at least 2d6. Also used for Grapple.

| BODY | 4 or less | 5–6 | 7–10 | 11+ |
|------|-----------|-----|------|-----|
| Damage | 1d6 | 2d6 | 3d6 | 4d6 |

## Grab

Action: grab/hold someone, take a held object, or escape a Grapple.

DEX + Brawling + 1d10 vs. DEX + Brawling + 1d10

Both in Grapple: -2 to all Actions. Defender cannot Move (dragged with Attacker). Attacker can end Grapple anytime; Defender (or other) must win a Grab to break it.

## Choke

If Attacker in Grapple: Action, no Check. BODY damage direct to HP (ignores armor). Choke same target 3 successive Rounds: Unconscious.

## Throw Object

Up to 25 m/yds. DEX + Athletics + 1d10 vs. DV (Grenade Launcher column). REF 8+ can dodge. Thrown melee weapons do not ignore 1/2 SP.

## Martial Arts

Requires Martial Arts Skill. ROF 2. Ignores 1/2 armor SP. Used for Martial Arts Attacks and Special Moves.

## Armor

Final Damage = Damage Roll − Armor SP. Melee Weapons and Martial Arts ignore 1/2 SP. If damage penetrates, armor SP −1.

## Explosives

Target a 10 m/yd square (not a person). All targets in that area take damage (roll once). REF 8+ can dodge. If cover is destroyed by the blast, targets behind it take full damage.

## Cover

In cover if fully behind something that could stop a bullet. If they have line of sight, you aren't in cover. No partial cover.

| Type of Cover | Thick HP | Thin HP |
|---------------|----------|---------|
| Steel | 50 | 25 |
| Stone | 40 | 20 |
| Bulletproof Glass | 30 | 15 |
| Concrete | 25 | 10 |
| Wood | 20 | 5 |
| Plaster/Foam/Plastic | 15 | 0 (Not Cover) |
