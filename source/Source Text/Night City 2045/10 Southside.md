City ManagerCity Manager
Bald as a bowling ball and known for his love of bright 
green suits, Elliot Kane  (Exec 5 ) is a gladhander. He 
started off as a minor Exec at Playland by the Sea, but 
ingratiated himself with every power player in the dis -
trict. When it came time to select a City Manager who 
would represent Playland but play nice with Tanson and 
the Jodes and the gangs, Kane was the only choice the 
powers that be agreed on.
When Kane isn’t taking meetings with leaders from 
Pacifica’s various factions, he’s walking through the district, 
greeting tourists and residents. He’s a charmer, sure, but 
sometimes comes off as a bit desperate to remind people of 
his importance. Time spent shaking hands and kissing butts 
helped Kane snag the job, but it won’t help him keep it. Last 
month, Pacifica lost power for almost an hour during peak 
hours at Playland by the Sea – a first in five years – and it 
cost the district’s overlords both time and money.
Word is, everyone and their grandmother summoned 
Kane to their office for a good chewing out. If it happens 
again, he might catch bullets instead of words. His bosses 
aren’t people who screw around.
PopulationPopulation
Most people living in Pacifica owe their livelihoods to 
Playland, Tanson, the Jodes, or one of the various broth -
els, casinos, or businesses dedicated to supporting the 
tourist trade. During peak vacation times, there are nearly 
as many tourists in the district as residents. Currently, the 
majority of those tourists come from other parts of the 
city, but as the NorCal Marshals increase patrols on the 
highways and make travel safer, visits from elsewhere in 
the Pacifica Confederation are becoming more common. 
Experts predict that it’ll be a 50/50 split by 2047 , espe -
cially if the Night City Spaceport opens on time.
We’ve also got the nomads from the Jodes Nation in 
force along the southern border, and a Korean cultural 
hub centered around the Tanson Group Headquarters, 
though neither group is interested in performing for tour -
ists. As for gangs, you can’t throw a stone in the district 
without hitting a Piranha, and the Voodoo Boys are 
always lurking around.Security ProviderSecurity Provider
In Night City, you can’t have high society without having 
high security! For the longest time, NCPD kept the peace 
exclusively, but now they’ve been joined by Militech. This 
didn’t make city cops happy, but they aren’t willing to 
start trouble with heavily armed corporate troopers in the 
middle of a crowded neighborhood. Both groups stop 
at the border of Tanson’s complex and the Jodes Camp. 
The nomads and the Korean megacorp provide their own 
security. 
Recent HistoryRecent History
Playland by the Sea reopened in 2036. Tanson moved 
into the district in 2040. The Jodes moved out of Santo 
Domingo after the Badlands Incident and set up camp 
in Pacifica in 2041. Combined, these three events mark 
the big sea-change moments that stopped the blight of 
Rancho Coronado from growing beyond its border and 
re-established Pacifica as the playground of Northern 
California.
Not that it's been all honey and wine. NCPD seemed 
either unwilling or unable to clamp down on the violence 
stemming from the war between the Piranhas and the 
Voodoo Boys, so earlier this year, the City Manager hired 
Militech to patrol the streets. Local companies followed 
suit, but since they can’t afford megacorp-level protection, 
many have hired budget Militech – aka 6th Street – to 
guard their establishments.

#2 Bits’n’Bolts
Security Code 6 : Militech, Hardened Light
Bits’n’Bolts is where district workers go when they need a 
replacement tool or a piece of technology fixed. Patchz  
(Tech 3 ), the Tech who runs the place, is simple and honest. 
If he says a job will take two days, he means it. If you need 
something faster, he’s willing to do a quick fix, but doesn’t 
pretend it’ll hold for long before your device breaks again. 
Bits’n’Bolts also sells salvage collected by scavvers from 
local construction sites. Check the scrolling marquee above 
the counter for the day’s deals when you stroll in.
#3 Cubeland by the Sea
Security Code 5 : Militech, Hardened Light
A cube hotel built for construction workers before the war, 
and it shows. Flatpack furniture in shambles, leaks that have 
never been fixed … hell, I’m convinced it leans hard any 
time a stiff breeze blows by. Some Playland by the Sea staff live here, but most residents are park enthusiasts who 
moved in because it's near enough to qualify for a discount 
ticket. The cheap rent gives them more to spend at the park.
#4 Dream Forest Development
Security Code 10 : Tanson Security, Medium
Some Pacifica residents mockingly refer to this mammoth 
structure as H0, though it isn’t nearly as large as a true 
Megabuilding. The seven-story, multi-block building is 
home to most of Tanson’s Night City employees and is 
designed to be as self-sufficient as possible – a near-ar -
cology where they can shop and live between shifts. 
The development’s hub is on the basement level. Called 
The Roots of the Forest , it is the closest thing to a func -
tioning pre-4CW mall you’ll find in Night City: restau -
rants, shops, spas, arcades, and art galleries. My favorites 
are the dueling bodysculpting clinics: SykoStyles  and 
EverSmilE . Each week, the two compete in a "face off" 
by presenting a fully sculpted customer and letting the 
gathered audience vote on which is more beautiful. Or, at 
least, that’s what I know from their shared Garden Patch. 
The truth is, I’ve never been inside. You aren’t allowed unless 
you’re a Tanson employee, family member, or an invited guest 
… and no one’s ever invited me. I can tell you, from gossip I’ve 
heard in the Ascension, that the first six above-ground levels 
are dedicated to residential units, though each floor also has 
a bodega, playground, and lounge space. 
The seventh floor is a massive, indoor park, complete 
with a swimming pool and a small golf course. Access to 
the park isn’t automatic. Employees (and their associated 
family members and guests) may only visit if their most 
recent monthly performance review "exceeds expecta -
tions." The Mudang Gumi are known to operate out of 
Dream Forest, but they don’t exactly advertise. No one is 
sure which residents are members of the digital theft ring.

#6 Jodes Camp
Security Code 7 : Jodes, Hardened Light
It isn’t as big as the Aldecaldo Camp in Santo Domingo, 
but I’d argue the Jodes Camp is just as lively. Essentially 
an oversized cargo container community with an adjoin -
ing trailer park, the camp is home base for nomads who 
belong to the Jodes Nation – a place where convoys can 
stop, drop off/pick up cargo, refuel, and resupply before 
they drive back out onto the nomad roads to the south 
and east. If you stop by the Camp, be sure to check out a 
few of the following:
◊ Doc Miles : I know you might be wary of visiting a 
ripperdoc who sits his clients down in an old dentist’s 
chair situated in a patched-up tent, but trust me, Doc 
Miles  (Medtech 4 ) is the best – and not just because he 
shares his tequila with his patients! He and his appren -
tices can install any chrome you purchase from the 
Nomad’s Market next door, clean and neat as can be.
◊ The Nomad’s Market : A market built out of five 
side-by-side containers. Whatever the Jodes haven’t 
already earmarked for sale to specific Fixers and 
shops is sold here. That includes food, weapons, gear, 
and clothing. If you’re interested in acquiring a Diego Motors vehicle, talk to Ojo (Fixer 6 ) in container three. 
It may take a while to arrive, but he’s your best bet to 
secure one. In the meantime, he and his crew can fix 
or upgrade your existing vehicle.
◊ The Ring : A retired Solo named Old Grace  (Nomad 
3/Solo 5 ) runs this martial arts school, specializing in 
Sumo and Wrestling. Most of her students are nomads 
looking to brush up on their skills. Non-Jodes who want 
to learn must impress Old Grace with a feat of stamina. 
I know one guy who ran the length of the city, from the 
top of Watson to the Jodes camp, to earn a place in 
her class. 
#7 Mister Rice Guy
Security Code 7 : Militech, Hardened Light
Mister Rice Guy is the place to go when you’re jonesing 
for a real fish fix. Not kidding! They have an authentic 
supply. Walk in, and you’re part of the machine itself, 
moving from screen to screen to push meals along the 
conveyor belts. Everything’s labeled by color, and what 
you get depends on what you’re willing to spend. From 
Yellow (South and East Asian flavors of Kibble for 10eb) 
to Pink (real fish, rice, and vegetables in the form of sushi 
or bowls for 200eb). Eat in, and you’ll be messaged by 
the restaurant’s digital mascot, Hime Cat, with a Lucky 
Treat fortune. Take-out meals are fortune-free.

#9 Pacifica Arcology Ruins
Security Code ? : ??, ??
The wreck of a building sticking out of the ocean down the 
shore? That’s the old Pacifica Arcology. It isn’t in Pacifica 
proper, but a frequent topic of bored speculation among 
residents. I hear it was originally a self-sufficient sanctu -
ary for investors fleeing the financial collapse of Hong 
Kong. Before the 4th Corporate War, they kept mostly to 
themselves, but occasionally people would emerge. After 
the nuke, they sealed the place off. No one’s heard from 
the residents since. Occasionally, scavver crews travel out 
there, hoping to break inside and strike it rich. None ever 
return. A few months ago, part of the southwest corner 
collapsed into the ocean. Since then, blobs of … some -
thing … all fleshy and toxic have begun washing up on 
shore in Pacifica. I personally recommend steering clear.
#10 Playhouse
Security Code 6 : Militech, Hardened Light
Playhouse is an apartment complex with a history. Not the 
most fun history, but, yeah, a history nonetheless. Once upon 
a time, DMS filmed a reality show called Playhouse  here. 
The ratings were killer … and so were the stars. The show’s 
been off the air for years now, and the landlord insists all the 
hidden cameras are gone, and the murder elevator from the 
finale has been replaced. Makes you feel safe, doesn’t it?
I lived there for less than a month, and I do not regret 
paying the fee I was hit with when I moved out before my 
rental agreement expired. Between the cold spots and the 
ghostly wails in the night, I decided, nope, it was time to 
go. I’d rather sleep in a damn combat zone with the door 
unlocked than spend another night in that place.
#1 1 Pleasant Valley Apartments
Security Code 8 : Militech, Hardened Light
Eight stories of minimalistic heaven, if you’re into retro, low-
tech living. The building employs no NET Architecture, features 
analog locks, and uses manual controls for lighting, climate 
control, and water. Even the elevator is old-fashioned. You 
actually have to push buttons to get where you want to go! Half the tenants live there not because they’re neo-lud -
dites, but because they really dig the pre-Collapse nos -
talgia vibe: corded phones stuck to the walls, fridges with 
dial-based temperature control, and televisions with no 
remote controls. 
Me? I think it’d get old real fast, but I guess someone 
must enjoy it, since the units are still selling. 
#1 2 Pursuit Security Inc Showroom
Security Code 8 : Militech, Hardened Light
Pursuit Security maintains a small showroom in Pacifica, 
stocked with goods brought in by the Jodes from the main 
factory in Southern California. This is the best place to 
come in Night City if you’re looking for the latest in "less 
than lethal" weaponry.
#13 Quaid Winston Memorial  
De-Salinization Plant
Security Code 8 : Hydrosubsidium Security, Heavy
It’s hard not to laugh a bit at the name of this place if you 
know the story behind it. Quaid Winston was an engineer 
on the project and won a lottery to drink the first glass of 
water it produced. Poor guy. Turns out the plant wasn’t 
working right. He gulped it down and quenched his thirst 
with a glass full of unpurified ocean water. 
Hydrosubsidium, the project’s sponsor, claimed it was 
sabotage and covered up the whole thing. Quaid’s family 
was placated with a small cash payment and a memorial 
plaque. The plant functions fine today, though most of the 
water goes to Playland by the Sea and not the residents 
of Pacifica.

#17 Stop. Shop. Stitch.
Security Code 8 : Militech, Hardened Light
This is a classic enemies to lovers story. Ruth Blue

#18 Tanson Group Headquarters
Security Code 10 : Tanson Security, Heavy
Tanson Group, a Korean megacorp, arrived in Night City 
five years ago and carved out a nice chunk of southern 
Pacifica to call home. The sprawling complex comprises 
five concrete domes, each with a purpose: administration, 
manufacturing (two domes), research and development, 
and warehousing. 
It took Tanson time to fully set up shop in Night City. 
The headquarters and residential area of Dream Forest 
Development were completed only two years ago. 
Employees were shipped in from Korea – most survived 
the voyage – and hired locally to staff the facility, but 
goods only began flowing out earlier this year. 
Curiously, little of it has been sent to stores in Night 
City. While Tanson products, ranging from food to vehi -
cles, have appeared for sale in other West Coast cities, 
the megacorp doesn’t seem interested in competing with 
Night City’s already-established corporate players. That 
might change as Tanson’s operation continues to grow. 
Word is, the company is looking to fill the void left by the 
destruction of IEC.
Tanson recently purchased an Advisory Seat on the City 
Council, so has the right to claim its facilities as self-po -
liced territory. Its security forces refuse to cooperate with 
NCPD or Militech officers, and visitors without proper 
authorization are captured, interrogated, and escorted off 
the property. Sometimes. Sometimes they just disappear. 
Their claim extends to the air as well. Tanson maintains a 
no-fly zone over its territory. Anti-aircraft guns on the roof 
of each dome will fire on any drones or vehicles flying 
overhead.

#18 Torta-sienda
Security Code 7 : Militech, Hardened Light
Torta-sienda is the epitome of an undiscovered hole-in-
the-wall eatery. I’ll admit, I’ve got a soft spot for their sign: 
a cute, sandwich-shaped turtle with a chili pepper for a 
tail, but the food is genuinely some of the best in the dis -
trict. Paz Moreno  runs the kitchen while his wife, Renata , 
handles the front of the house. The family takes pride in its 
food and uses its connections to the Jodes to source fresh 
ingredients. Paz can’t eliminate SCOP entirely, but he can 
minimize its use.
Paz and Renata’s son, Miguel, works in Playland by the 
Sea as a mobile restaurant. If you’re tight with him, he’ll 
teach you a special hand sign. Show it to Renata, and 
she’ll treat you like family, complete with discount. 
#19 Volkodav Racetrack
Security Code 9 : Voodoo Boys, Medium
The universe must like balance. Why else would we have 
the Ascension offering upscale gambling on one side of 
the district and Volkodav offering a lower-class version of 
the same thing on the other side?
Volkodav runs betting on vintage stuffed rabbit-on-a-
pole type dog races. The rabbits are plastic cut-outs, and 
the dogs are beat-up old Adrek Patrol Units – the kind 
of robotic canine that NCPD stopped deploying twenty 
years ago. Races run 24/7 , and the minimum bet is 5eb. 
You can buy Smash for 10eb a can. If you’re more inter -
ested in burning your brain instead of gambling, XBDs 
are available for rent in the Party Room , a large empty 
space covered in dirty mattresses.
With the Russian name, you’d think the Consortium 
runs the joint, but that’s not the case. The Voodoo Boys 
moved into Volkodav a few years back. Yes, they profit 
from the races, but the real action happens underground. 
The gang cooks and stores drugs – as well as joytoys and 
XBD "stars" from Rancho Coronado – in the basement.

tion wafting up through the track to be an enhancement to

Security Code 8 : 6th Street, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 4
Before the 4th Corporate War, Minimallism was bea -
verville nirvana. A temple to the three Cs: commerce, 
consumption, and capital. After the nuke, the building 
was abandoned and then squatted in. Now, Fixers infest 
the carcass. Go inside Minimallism, and you’ll find the 
following:
◊ 6th Street Station : A conglomerate of Fixers who run 
Night Markets out of Minimallism recently pooled their 
resources to hire 6th Street as security. The Pickens 
Squad operates out of this station, built into an old 
electronics store.◊ RC Night Market : Anyone with an ounce of street 
savvy knows Night Markets aren’t a permanent deal. 
Fixers set them up, they sell out, and then they close 
up. Only the big rollers have enough stock to keep one 
going for more than a couple hours. The thing is, the 
Minimallism is so big and convenient that half a dozen 
different Fixers are all running their own markets there 
on any given week, and the prices are low enough 
to attract customers from outside Rancho Coronado. 
The mall’s most prominent regular Fixer, Ms. Mynah  
(Fixer 6 ), runs an apparel-oriented Night Market on 
the 5th of each month, but maintains a permanent 
office off the mall’s central plaza. Don’t stare too long 
at her gorgeous bird-themed exotic sculpt, her body -
guard considers it to be rude.
◊ The Samaritan : Sara Snow  (Medtech 3/Tech 2 ) 
is crazy. I just have to say that. The Samaritan is her 
clinic, run out of a former bodysculpting beauty shop, 
and she sometimes offers discounts, a concept alien 
to most ripperdocs. The wait times are insane, though. 
Every once in a while, someone dies before they get 
seen, but that’s life in Rancho Coronado. Here’s the 
catch: when I said Sara Snow was crazy, I meant it. 
She’s obsessed with the idea of concocting a universal 
panacea capable of curing every disease, so anyone 
who accepts a discount does so by agreeing to be a 
test monkey for her latest serum. If you visit, you risk 
Sara’s latest cure being worse than the disease.
◊ XOOX : You won’t find an Oasis in Rancho Coronado, 
but you can find a close approximation in XOOX, a 
bodega run out of Minimallism’s abandoned food 
court. XOOX is run by members of the Steel Vaqueros’ 
extended family and stocked with whatever goods the 
nomad pack drops off when they visit Night City.
