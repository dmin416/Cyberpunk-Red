Jericho Hunt (JH) : Playland by the Sea is hiding something

The Happiest Place in Night CityThe Happiest Place in Night City

JH: Playland by the Sea is owned and operated by a 
SoCal group named Tower of Dark. The park is also 
home to multiple Piranha shoals and a disturbing number 
of yogangs. Why does Playland management allow 
gangs to infest their park? Because the Piranhas are an 
investment. If the party gang claims Playland as territory, 
it protects the park from nastier, more violent ones. The 
yogangs are basically slave labor. I’ll cover that later.

Park ManagementPark Management
Working behind the scenes to help make your Playland 
Playcation a dreamlike experience, our park management is

JH: With an unlimited supply of Surge and unlimited

These people aren’t dangerous – unless they’ve got a fish

When paying guests ask about the packs of kids running 
around Playland, park staff always respond, "Playland 
by the Sea is so safe, parents let their children run free!" 
That’s a lie. Most of the kids you see "running free" in the 
park either don’t have parents or ran away from them. 
They made their way through the worst parts of Night City, 
snuck into the park, and live inside now.
The Piranhas offer some measure of protection to the 
kids living in the park, so management isn’t hauling them 
out by the truckload. Instead, Playland’s overlords put the 
kids to work. As long as they help keep the park going, 
they’re allowed to make homes in the hidden corners.
Over the years, the kids in Playland by the Sea have 
organized into yogangs. Membership is fluid, as kids jump 
from one group to another based on taste, relationships, 
and interests. Kids who age out of a gang don’t have 
much in the way of choice. Many are recruited into the 
Piranhas, and some score jobs working at Playland for 
actual money. Others find they’re no longer welcome in 
the park at all, and are exiled to the outside world.
