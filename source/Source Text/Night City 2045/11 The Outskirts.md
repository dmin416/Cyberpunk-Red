The OutskirtsThe Outskirts
Security Code 5: Sheriff, Hardened Light ◊ ◊ Utility Code: Variable
The real Night City doesn’t stop at the city limits. What happens in the Outskirts – the area surrounding the city 
– can be just as important as what happens in any overpacked suburb, combat zone, or rebuilding urban center. 
Let me introduce myself again. My name is Trace Santiago  (Media 6 ). I’m an independent Media, but I’m 
also a proud member of the Aldecaldo nomad nation. As a nomad, I’ve spent a great deal of time in the desert 
surrounding Night City. As someone on the outside of the corporate structure that runs this monstrosity of a 
metropolis, you probably will, too. Let me tell you about it.
Leave Night City, and you’ll enter the badlands: hard, bare ground, stunted vegetation, and cracked roads 
as far as the eye can see. It wasn’t always like this. I’m told the area used to be lush and green, with grass and 
woodlands, but, well, nature’s been going downhill since the Collapse of the 1990s, and California’s natural 
deserts have grown year by year to cover much of the State.The Outskirtsby Claudio Pozasby Claudio Pozas

That isn’t to say the Outskirts are devoid of life or tech -
nology. There are some plants and animals hardy enough 
to survive on harsh, unfiltered sunlight and minimal water. 
Wind turbines dot the landscape, generating power to 
send back to Night City, and stubborn, resourceful people 
gather in enclaves, seeking to exist on their own terms 
beyond the city line.
City ManagerCity Manager
The area around Night City is part of San Luis Obispo 
County, but no one really governs it. If you want to live in 
the Outskirts legally, you need to drive to the county seat, 
San Luis Obispo, a reclaimer town southeast of Night City. 
Otherwise, just stake a claim.
PopulationPopulation
Believe it or not, most folk living in the Outskirts do so 
by choice: reclaimers, hermits, farmers, and independent 
thinkers. While their reasons vary, from classic "pioneer 
spirit" to outright paranoia, the people you’ll meet out 
here are tough. They have to be in order to survive the 
landscape, the weather, and Raffen Shiv raids.
Security ProviderSecurity Provider
The San Luis Obispo County Sheriff’s office provides tradi -
tional law enforcement and investigation services outside 
of Night City, but is so poorly staffed and underfunded 
that you’re better off relying on your shotgun and your 
neighbors than the authorities. Truth be told, most of the 
deputies working in the department are bullies anyway. 
The type who wanted the benefits of a gun and a badge, 
but couldn’t pass the qualification tests for the NCPD.
NorCal Marshals, specifically the Highway Patrol, 
monitor and enforce the law on major thoroughfares 
both around and inside Night City, but rarely interfere 
with off-road matters.
Recent HistoryRecent History
The Outskirts are more a place people drive through on 
the way to history than a place where history happens. 
Violence does occur, of course, including Raffen Shiv 
raids and highway crashes, but no one is gossiping about 
it. Ask the people who live out here about recent events, and they’re more likely to tell you about a birth, a cookout, 
or who’s fucking who than they are about the Pol-Bud 
Cement Factory celebrating five years of existence or a 
plane crashing at the airport. Big news is for city folk. 
People in the Outskirts prefer gossip.

#1 107 .3 Morro Rock Radio
Security Code 9 : Orbital Air Security, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 3
Morro Rock Radio began life as a volunteer gig, with

#2 Independent Farms
Security Code 6 : Sheriff, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 4
Most of the food production in North America is done on 
massive, corporate-controlled farms in more hospitable parts 
of the country, but a few determined individuals and families 
in the Outskirts are giving it a go. Most of the small, inde -
pendent farms outside of Night City are part of Biotechnica’s 
TradFarm Initiative – essentially a franchise model where 
Biotechnica acts as both the exclusive provider of seeds, live -
stock, and supplies and as the primary distributor of the end 
product. It isn’t the best deal, but Biotechnica’s involvement 
prevents Continental Brands from sending out troops to raze 
the farms to the ground like it does with most competition.
◊  A. Edgewood Farm : A dairy farm owned by the Harris 
family. Despite extensive tinkering by Biotechnica, the 
cows are not thriving in the modern California environ -
ment. Multiple turrets protect the barn and house.
◊ B. Ingalls Farm : A small farm specializing in vegetables. 
The Ingalls have been known to skim from the harvest and 
sell on the side to certain high-end restaurants in Night 
City – a violation of their contract with Biotechnica.

◊ C. Poppy Farm : Owned by Jack Boles  (Exec 3 ). The 
Poppy Farm is well named, since it grows a breed of 
Papaver somniferum , aka the opium poppy. Boles’ 
crop has been genetically modified by Biotechnica to 
withstand the virus that wiped out most of the species 
during the 1990s. The farm is of great importance 
to the megacorp, so it stations guards here full time 
(Security Code 10 : Biotechnica Security, Medium) .
#3 Jackson Plains Regional Airport
Security Code 9 : Aldecaldo Peacekeepers, Hardened Light   
Utility Code : 4
Following the 4th Corporate War, the Scannelli-Odujinrin 
clan of Highriders descended to Earth, built a network 
of compact regional airports across North America, and 
recruited Folk Nation pilots to fly between them. 
The chief administrator of the airport closest to Night 
City is Sionnach Taylor  (Nomad 7 ), a classic rocket 
jockey who squeezes races in his beat-up old Thunderbird 
AV-9 in between logistics and scheduling meetings. He 
remains optimistic for the future, insisting Night City will 
still need a lower-budget regional airport even when the 
Spaceport is up and operational. 
Passengers looking to fill their bellies or wet their whis -
tles while they wait for a flight can stop in the Calbraith 
Diner , a classic bar/greasy spoon. Security at the airport 
is provided by Aldecaldo Peacekeepers. Crew aboard all 
Folk Nation-run aircraft are armed, but cannot guarantee 
your safety. Bring a gun, just in case.
#4 Laguna Bend
Security Code 7 : Sheriff, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 4
A small town built on the edge of the Laguna Reservoir, 
Laguna Bend has become a symbol to reclaimers in the 
region of what is possible when community and persever -
ance merge to create something new.
The vibe of Laguna Bend hearkens back to pre-Col -
lapse life in America. Real houses. Neighbors who trust 
one another and work together. Regular community events 
held in the town square. That sort of thing.Of course, this is 2045, and not even small-town life 
can be all sunshine and beauty. The community stays 
well-armed, ready to fight off raiders and Night City 
gangers looking to raise hell. Illnesses are common, 
especially considering the pollutant-carrying dust storms 
that regularly sweep through town, and tragedy still strikes 
with disturbing frequency. A good example is the school 
shooting that occurred here last year. Four kids and two 
teachers died. The town is still recovering.
Points of interest in Laguna Bend include:
◊ Laguna Bend Public School : A blended school with 
two wings. Elementary and middle school students go 
to classes in the east wing. High school students go to 
classes in the west wing. After last year’s shooting, the 
community hired 6th Street to guard the school.
◊ Flo’s Diner : The only restaurant in Laguna Bend. The 
burgers are big, greasy, and cooked so well you can’t 
even tell they’re SCOP.
◊ Fuel Station : A rare independent CHOOH2 station. 
Laguna Bend residents buy most of their daily supplies 
from the attached bodega.
◊ Saint Catherine’s : The local church and community 
center. Most of the town’s religiously inclined citizens 
are Catholic.
#5 Municipal Landfill 
Security Code 8 : Petrochem/SovOil Security, Medium ◊  Utility Code : 5
The constant rebuilding of Night City in the Time of the Red 
has produced tons of garbage. Initially, much of it was 
pushed into the harbor, but as time went on, it became clear 
continued ocean dumping would limit shipping, so the City 
Council designated a site outside the city limits as Night 
City’s Municipal Landfill. The contract to operate the landfill 
was awarded jointly to Coastal Carting (a subsidiary of 
Petrochem) and SMKP Hauling (a subsidiary of SovOil). Both 
outfits keep a fleet of TetraCorp America PowerLoad Refuse 
Trucks on site, as well as facilities to store and maintain them.
When Petrochem and SovOil personnel at the Landfill 
grow bored with shouting at one another, they gear up 
and hunt down the scavvers that pick through the trash.

#6 Night City Spaceport
Security Code 10 : Orbital Air Security, Hardened Heavy ◊  Utility Code : 2
It turns out, Richard Night’s plans for Night City (then 
Coronado City) weren’t complete when he died. Among 
his unfulfilled dreams was a spaceport, complete with 
mass driver, ready to launch the goods produced and 
shipped through the city into orbit.
In 2035, Night Corp decided to complete its found -
er’s dream and pushed the City Council to offer Orbital 
Air a sweetheart deal, essentially giving Morro Rock 
— and other considerations — to the megacorp if it 
promised to establish a mass driver there. Orbital Air 
agreed and went to work. Or pretended to, anyway. It 
turns out Morro Rock’s geology made it unsuitable for 
a mass driver. Instead, Orbital Air built a more standard 
rocket and delta-based spaceport. By the time Night 
Corp figured things out, it was too late. Orbital Air was 
almost done with construction. To save face, Night 
Corp and the Council pretended this was the plan all 
along and quietly buried all mentions of a mass driver 
from press materials. Night City Spaceport (NCX) is 
scheduled to open sometime in the next three years.
About a thousand people currently live on and around 
Morro Rock. The vast majority of them are construction 
laborers and low-level engineers – flown in via shuttle 
from orbit or from Africa – who reside in float homes and 
cube hotels. Mid-ranking staff live in a temporary hotel 
officially designated as Orbital Air Housing Block #1  
while high-level employees rent nice conapts in the city.
Nomad Campsites
Security Code 8 : Nomads, Medium ◊  Utility Code : Various
Not all nomad groups stay in the large Night City 
camps. Those only visiting the area for a short time often 
bypass the city altogether and find a good spot of land 
in the Outskirts to make camp. If you approach a nomad 
camp, be cautious. Go in with your hands visible and 
your weapons holstered. Most nomads won’t shoot on 
sight as long as you aren’t an obvious threat. I recom -
mend bringing a gift to show your sincerity and peaceful 
intentions.#7 NorCal Marshal Station
Security Code 8 : NorCal Marshals, Hardened Heavy ◊  Utility Code : 3
The NorCal Marshal Service maintains a mid-sized station 
outside of Night City. It serves primarily as a base of opera -
tions for the Highway Patrol, but a squad of Rangers can be 
found here when they aren’t out hunting fugitives. Marshal 
Inspectors sent to the area usually borrow workspace in 
one of NCPD’s precinct houses. The Station is heavily for -
tified and surrounded by thick, razor wire-covered walls. 
Turrets at each corner of the compound discourage raiders.
#8 Militech Ballistics and Explosives Range
Security Code 3 : Militech, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 5
Militech isn’t stupid enough to run large-scale live fire/
explosive exercises in the city. Instead, its employees travel 
north into the Outskirts and blow up shit there, instead! 
The area is clearly marked by signposts. I recommend 
staying far away. Even if training isn’t in progress, it is still 
dangerous since unexploded munitions litter the place. The 
Estero Bay Military COG, NCPD, and other security firms 
rent the Range out from time to time for training.
#9 Night Corp Solar Station
Security Code 9 : Lazarus, Hardened Medium ◊  Utility Code : 2
After the dust thrown into the atmosphere by the 4th 
Corporate War finally settled, Night Corp teamed up with 
PacCon Electric and built a large solar array in the Outskirts 
to take advantage of all the energy shining down through 
the Earth’s patchy ozone layer. The Solar Station fulfills a 
substantial amount of Night City’s energy needs, so Night 
Corp isn’t skimping on security. Expect at least one squad 
of Lazarus troops – complete with drone surveillance – to 
be on patrol in the area at all times.

Petrochem already owned the mineral rights to much 
of the Del Coronado Bay area when it helped bankroll 
Richard Night’s dream of a corporate-friendly utopia.

With no pesky regulations keeping the megacorp from 
drilling to its greedy heart’s content, Petrochem began 
building oil rigs on the same day Richard Night’s golden 
shovel broke ground on Coronado City.
During the 2nd Corporate War, Petrochem leased 
some of its Night City oil fields to NorCal-based 
company Prudhoe Pride Oil as a way of raising funds 
for its battle against SovOil. The deal lasted until 202 1, 
when SovOil purchased Prudhoe Pride. Tension built 
between the two megacorps as Petrochem insisted on 
terminating Prudhoe Pride’s lease and SovOil insisted 
that an obscure clause buried deep in the contract actu -
ally granted it full ownership. This tension might have 
boiled over into a sequel to the 2nd Corporate War, but 
the oil-based grudge match was interrupted by Arasaka 
and Militech’s global conflict.
In the aftermath of the 4th Corporate War, Night Corp 
stepped in to broker an uneasy peace between Petrochem 
and SovOil. Everyone knows the Northern Oil Fields 
don’t have much time left, so the only reason to fight over 
them is pride. Each megacorp continues to operate the 
rigs under its control, intending to drain the fields dry. 
Squabbles between Petrochem and SovOil rig crews 
aren’t uncommon, but they rarely end in more than minor 
property damage and the occasional murdered worker.

#1 2 Pol-Bud Cement Factory
Security Code 7 : Pol-Bud Security, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 4
Pol-Bud – established in 201 3 by Franz and Tonya 
Yanovsky – found success in the SoCal construction mate -
rials market through a combination of competitive pricing, 
strict supply chain control, and low-cost labor. Pol-Bud 
imported this formula to Night City in 2035 and built a 
factory in the Outskirts, quickly growing into the largest 
cement supplier for the rebuilding metropolis.Pol-Bud believes firmly in maintaining complete 
control/ownership of as much of its supply chain as pos -
sible. Mines. Factories. Transport vehicles. Security forces. 
Even employee housing. All are owned, employed, and/
or operated by Pol-Bud.
#13 Pol-Bud Miasto
Security Code 5 : Pol-Bud Security, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 6
When Pol-Bud moved to the Outskirts, it didn’t just build 
industrial facilities. It also set up a town to house all of its 
workers. Or, at least, it set up a cargo community pretend -
ing to be a town. Everything in Pol-Bud Miasto is company 
owned, from the homes to the bar to the CHOOH2 station, 
bodega, and Polish Orthodox Chapel. Workers can pay 
cash if they want, but most charge their purchases to a 
company credit account. Both the balance and interest 
accrued are then garnished from their wages. 
Only workers live in Miasto. Pol-Bud’s Execs stay in nice 
conapts in the city. The biggest wig of them all, Regional 
Director Doctor Karen Davies, lives in the Executive Zone. 
#14 Pol-Bud Mine
Security Code 6 : Pol-Bud Security, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 4
Pol-Bud controls several mines across Northern and 
Southern California. The largest – currently – is a lime -
stone mine located in the Outskirts outside Night City.
The mine operates 24/7 using two shifts of workers, 
and the pace means accidents are common. Pol-Bud 
isn’t legally required to report statistics, but independent 
estimates suggest four to five deaths per month due to 
tunnel collapses, pit falls, and machinery malfunctions.
Raffen Shiv Campsites
Security Code 2 : Sheriff, Hardened Medium ◊  Utility Code : Various
Somewhere out there are the camps of various Raffen Shiv 
packs, such as the Toecutters. These are former nomads, 
exiled for breaking the code and living among these 
bandits are the worst monsters you can encounter in the 
Outskirts. Steer clear .

#1 5 Sheriff’s Station
Security Code 8 : Sheriff, Hardened Medium ◊  Utility Code : 4
The local San Luis Obispo County Sheriff’s Station is an 
underwhelming concrete structure perched in the middle of 
nowhere. The officers assigned to the station share space 
with their vehicles in the building’s large open space. The 
station is protected by concrete bollards topped with razor 
wire and a single turret.
#16 SovOil CHOOH2 Station
Security Code 6 : Sheriff, Hardened Light ◊  Utility Code : 5
A small SovOil CHOOH2 station located off the New Pacific 
Highway. It is attached to an equally small Oasis store.
#17 Sunshine Motel
Security Code 7 : Staff, Medium ◊  Utility Code : 3
Not every motorist on the Interstate journeys as part of a 
nomad convoy full of kombis. Lone travelers willing to brave 
the highways but unwilling to risk their lives by staying over -
night in Night City can pull into the Sunshine Motel. There, 
they can find a hall full of vendits to fill their belly and a 
clean bed behind a locked door to rest in for 20eb a night.
The motel is owned and operated by the Block clan. 
Ma Block  (Fixer 2 ) and her son Tony  (Solo 2 ) run the 
front counter. Most nights, at least one member of the clan 
sits on the roof with a rocket launcher, ready to blow the 
hell out of any Raffen Shiv riding by. The Blocks are pretty 
good shots and rarely hit guest vehicles with their rockets.
#18 Union Railroad Station
Security Code 7 : NorCal Marshals, Hardened Medium ◊  Utility Code : 4
Union Railroad Station serves as a major transfer point for 
trains carrying cargo in and out of Night City, making it a 
regular check-in spot for NorCal Marshals searching for 
smuggled goods or train robbers. 
The station’s operators, a Snake Nation nomad pack 
known as the Groundhog Gulpers, keep it running using 
computer systems that were antiquated even before the Collapse – making it impossible to subvert via netrunning. 
The Deadwoods maintain a supply depot and bunkhouse 
at the station, allowing members of the gang to rest 
between security runs.
