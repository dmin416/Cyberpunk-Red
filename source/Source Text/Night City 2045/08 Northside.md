Call me Thompson . No need for you to know my real name. Just know I’ve lived on base for over a decade, 
so the information I’m providing is accurate … to a point. I owe Trace Santiago’s old man a debt, so I’m writing 
up a quick tour, but I also believe in the oath I took and the independence of Northern California as a sovereign 
entity, so I won’t be sharing strategic information about troop numbers, ongoing operations, or strike targets – 
power plants, ammo and missile silos, POLCs (Petroleum, Oil, Lubricant, and CHOOH2 facilities), and the like. 
Be content with what you get and don’t dig further unless you want bullets in your skull and your chest.
The NorCal Military Base was officially established in 2016 by the treaty that recognized Northern California 
as a Free State. Originally, it housed units from all branches of the United States military. Both sides gained 
something from the deal. The 3rd Corporate War was raging at the time, so the United States government wanted 
more military assets on hand to protect against "accidental" corporate aggression on North American soil.NorCal Military Baseby Sebastian Szmydby Sebastian Szmyd

Meanwhile, the newly established NorCal government 
knew that a military base near the border would prevent 
Southern California from launching an invasion to reclaim 
territory.
Units from the base participated in the operational siege 
of Night City during the 4th Corporate War, joining other 
elements of the United States military in surrounding the 
city. After the nuke, base command remained loyal for a 
few years, but a lack of proper communication and devas -
tated supply lines highlighted the writing on the wall. If the 
NorCal Military Base wanted to survive, it needed to adapt 
to the Time of the Red so, in 2027 , the command staff for -
mally swore allegiance to Northern California, forming the 
Estero Bay Military Combined Operation Group (COG). 
City ManagerCity Manager
As a district, the NorCal Military Base is unique. It pays 
its fees for a Manager Seat on the council and votes on 
bills and other important matters, but in all other ways, it 
is an independent entity. Base law trumps Night City law 
here every time.
The Base Commander/City Manager is General Ash 
Giovanni  (Exec 7/Solo 6 ). I’ll spare you a detailed listing 
of promotions and decorations. Just know he started at rock 
bottom, as a Private First Class in the United States Army, 
earned his commission as an officer on the battlefield, 
and rose through the ranks about as far as you can go. 
He had two stars on his shoulder when the 4th Corporate 
War broke out. A third star was added after he arranged 
a switch of allegiance from the United States to NorCal. 
The General is a shrewd commander with an iron will. 
Which is to say he’s stubborn as hell. Where other men of 
his advanced years would be looking to retire, General 
Giovanni has refused to surrender to the infirmities of age. 
He uses neural implants to keep his brain sharp and a 
custom-designed external linear frame to stay the combat 
equal of any soldier on base.
PopulationPopulation
The majority of the base’s residents are the soldiers, 
sailors, and airmen of the Estero Bay Military COG. 
Most are assigned to one of four forces. In case you’re wondering, the names are legacy titles, carried over 
from the days when these outfits were part of the United 
States military.
◊ Task Force 384 : A naval group, organizing the defense 
of most of Northern California’s coast. The 384 works 
both independently and with local authorities in 
anti-piracy operations, and to keep NorCal’s harbors 
and ports safe from autonomous self-replicating mines 
left over from the 4th Corporate War.
◊ 157th Tactical Air Squadron : A fighter squadron that 
patrols half the State, from the NorCal border to the 
San Francisco/Tahoe line. The 1 57th primarily pro -
vides air support for the 223rd, but occasionally gets 
into good, old-fashioned dogfights with air pirates.
◊ 223rd Battalion : The Estero Bay Military COG’s 
ground forces. It fields tanks and other armored vehi -
cles on ready status, plus a detachment of infantry. The 
223rd patrols the State between the NorCal border 
and the SanFran/Tahoe line, keeping their combat 
skills sharp in engagements with Raffen Shiv and other 
roving marauders.
◊ 120.8th Engineer Battalion : The engineers and crews 
responsible for construction and maintenance on base, 
as well as aiding in various projects across Northern 
California. It also provides combat engineers to other 
forces to assist in field operations.
When the Estero Bay Military COG broke off from the 
United States, General Giovanni declared that no civil -
ians would reside on base. Everyone would be military, 
in some capacity or another. If you’re a civilian and move 
on base for a job or due to marriage, you are required by 
law and contract to enlist in the Estero Bay Military COG 
Reserve. COG Reservists receive regular training and must 
pick up arms and report for duty when called upon.
Children raised on base aren’t exempt, either. As soon 
as they are old enough for school, all COG "brats" join 
the CTCs (COG Training Corps), aka the "COG Scouts." 
Younger kids meet during the school day and on week -
ends to receive training in vital military skills, including 
leadership, personal defense, discipline, and teamwork.

Security ProviderSecurity Provider
As I noted before, base law trumps Night City law here, 
so security is provided by the Estero Bay Military COG 
Military Police (the COG MPs). They are trained to act as 
both soldiers and law enforcement officers, depending on 
the situation, and are accordingly armed. Anyone caught 
engaging in criminal activity on base is prosecuted in 
military court. Trials are quick, brief, and usually end in 
a conviction. Criminals found guilty of minor offenses are 
sentenced to time in the stockade, combined with heavy 
labor. Criminals found guilty of major offenses are occa -
sionally implanted with a proximity-activated cortex bomb 
that will blow if they try to enter the base and then exiled, 
but are more often lined up against a wall and shot. General Giovanni recently charged the MPs with the task of 
rooting out the Culper Ring, a secret society/gang composed 
of troops who believe NorCal should reunite with the United 
States. Word is, the Ring has repeatedly leaked sensitive intel -
ligence to United States agents elsewhere in Night City.
Recent HistoryRecent History
The Estero Bay Military COG is at war. It isn’t one we declared, 
and we do not battle a specific nation, but we are at war nev -
ertheless. Specifically, we are at war with the Time of the Red. 
Our troops are not content to sit behind the walls of the base, 
idly waiting for conflict to break out between nations. Instead, 
we seek to keep Northern California safe by patrolling the 
waters, skies, and land of our Free State and destroying the 
forces that would prey on its citizens. This war has a real cost. 
In the past year alone, we’ve suffered the following losses.
◊ NCS Alchesay , a Moray-class fighting submersible. 
Destroyed by an autonomous self-replicating mine in 
the Pacific. Four crew members killed in action.
◊ Three Militech Gorgon Security Vans. Destroyed by 
hostile forces while on patrol near Fresno. Fifteen 
soldiers killed in action.
◊ One Militech Gyrfalcon Gunship. Shot down over the 
Great Basin Desert during a battle with a Raffen Shiv 
clan. Two airmen and six soldiers killed in action.
◊ Two Militech Commando ACPA units. Destroyed while 
putting down a riot at New Alcatraz prison. Two sol -
diers killed in action.
◊ NCS Alaska , a Riptide class littoral combat ship. Lost 
at sea. Seventy-five crew members missing in action.

#2 COG Treatment Facility (CTF)
Security Code 10 : COG MPs, Hardened Heavy
This is the base hospital. While the staff and technology 
here aren’t the equal of Trauma Team’s client-only hospital, 
it provides care better than or equal to most civilian medical 
facilities. The Medtechs here are especially skilled in treating 
wounds sustained in combat. The burn ward, especially, is 
second to none.
The CTF provides free cyberware to troops, so long as a 
commanding officer has signed off on the procedure. All base 
personnel are regularly evaluated for cyberpsychosis. Therapy, 
and even cyberware extraction, is mandatory for those at risk.
#3 Estero Bay Military COG Academy
Security Code 10 : COG MPs, Hardened Medium
The Academy is a combined educational facility that pro -
vides training and care for every child on base. Elementary 
students attend classes on the first and second floors. The 
third floor is designated for middle school classes, and high 
school students attend classes on the fourth and fifth floors.
In addition to serving as an institution of learning, the 
Academy is also an emergency safeguard point and is 
accordingly reinforced. In case of a natural disaster, such 
as a typhoon, or an attack, such as a missile strike, non-es -
sential personnel can take shelter there. The Academy is 
built on an elevator system, allowing the entire building 
to sink beneath the ground if the situation warrants it. Thus 
far, that feature has only been activated once, in 2041, 
when a software glitch convinced the Academy’s SAAI 
that a nuclear device had detonated in Night City.
#4 Fire & Emergency Response Center
Security Code 10 : COG MPs, Hardened Medium
Part of the 1 20.8th Engineer Battalion, the personnel of 
Fire and Emergency Response Center are fully ready and 
able to respond to any non-combat crisis, including fires, 
hazardous material disasters, and medical emergencies.The Response Center houses a small but capable fleet 
of response vehicles, including Night City’s only AV 
pumper truck, as well as multiple ground and air-based 
ambulances.
#5 Main Gate
Security Code 10 : COG MPs, Hardened Heavy
The base’s main gate is located on Sir Frances Drake Avenue. 
It consists of a fortified gatehouse and a small lot where 
visitors can park while credentials are checked by the MPs 
stationed there. This is the only entry/exit point available 
to outsiders. Additional gates are located on other roads 
leading into the base, but are always closed and guarded.
Militech has its own gates, on Mary E. Walker Street 
and Norton Drive, guarded by its personnel, allowing 
access to its headquarters and employee housing.
#6 Mess Hall
Security Code 9 : COG MPs, Hardened Medium
The NorCal Military Base Mess Hall is the single largest 
purveyor of prepared food in Night City. Each day, it 
provides thousands of meals to hungry soldiers, sailors, 
airmen, and reservists. The food is almost entirely Kibble 
and SCOP, but it fills the belly and fuels the furnace. 
Officers have the option of eating dinner at the Officers’ 
Club, located in the USO Hall, where fresh food is avail -
able for a reduced price.

forces.

between the lobby and a garage, housing response vehi -
cles — primarily specially converted VMW Aerojeeps, 
along with two heavily armed Militech Gorgon Security 
Vans for the rare tactical operation. The second and 
third floors house offices, meeting rooms, the armory,

#8 Militech Corporate Housing
Security Code 10 : Militech, Hardened Medium
Militech houses roughly half of its workforce at a facility 
located near the base’s main gate. The rooms are spartan,

All guests must undergo a thorough security check

#9 Militech Offices
Security Code 10 : Militech, Hardened Heavy
Militech’s Pacifica Confederation headquarters may seem 
underwhelming at first, since the seven-story building, done 
in a harsh modern corporate style, seems too small for the 
literal embodiment of the military-industrial complex. Step 
into an elevator inside, however, and you’ll notice the 
touchpad has designations for twenty different sublevels.
That is, honestly, the extent of what I know for sure about 
the building. I have only been inside once, while working 
with Militech on promotional material for the base. I was 
escorted to my counterpart’s office when I arrived, then 
escorted out when we finished our tasks. Scuttlebutt tells 
me the building, both above and below ground, contains 
an assortment of rooms, including conference suites, rec -
reational facilities, infirmaries, cafeterias, firing ranges, 
an auditorium, a state-of-the-art war room, a Tech lab, 
an AV garage, and security suites. Security is heavy on 
every floor.Visitors rarely enter the main building. Instead, they meet 
with Militech employees in an ancillary facility located 
just inside the Mary E. Walker Street gate. The meeting 
facility also houses a showroom, where Militech takes 
select clients for demonstrations and purchases.
Militech’s offices are walled off from the rest of the 
NorCal Military Base. Travel further into the base requires 
passing through a checkpoint guarded by NorCal Military 
MPs.
#10 Motor Pool
Security Code 1 1 : COG MPs, Hardened Heavy
Calling the base’s Motor Pool a garage would be like 
calling War and Peace  a light summer read. This is 
where the 223rd Battalion’s tanks, armored vehicles, 
and ACPAs are stored and maintained. Much as with 
the 1 57th, supply chain issues forced the 223rd to add 
to their fleet piecemeal, meaning the Motor Pool houses 
an assortment of vehicles, including MT-4 battle tanks, 
Behemoth armored personnel carriers, and a surprising 
number of Arasaka Combat 10s captured after the 4CW. 
The 223rd’s pride and joy, though, is a squad’s worth of 
shiny new Militech Commando Assisted Combat Personal 
Armor (ACPA) units.
Also, the Motor Pool actually can act as a regular garage. 
If a resident’s personal vehicle is broken, the mechanics 
here will repair it for a small fee. The fee is usually waived if 
someone has the right rank on their uniform.
#1 1 The Oaks
Security Code 9 : COG MPs, Hardened Medium
A district within a district, the Oaks is a neighborhood and 
home to the majority of base residents. Exactly where you 
live depends on your status as a member of the COG. 
New recruits and unmarried enlisted live in one of a 
series of group barracks, housing between ten to one 
hundred people each. Barracks are co-ed, and there are 
strict rules about fraternization. Unmarried officers live in 
UOQs (Unescorted Officers Quarters), buildings divided 
into cube hotel-style rooms for lower-ranking officers and 
studio apartments for mid-ranking officers.

#1 2 Parade Ground
Security Code 9 : COG MPs, Hardened Medium

When the Parade Ground isn’t in use by the troops, it

#13 The Potentilla
Security Code 1 1 : COG MPs, Hardened Heavy
The Potentilla is named for its shape: five buildings 
arranged like the petals of a flower around a central 
courtyard. Four of the buildings are assigned, one each, 
to act as headquarters for Task Force 384, the 1 57th 
Tactical Air Squadron, the 223rd Battalion, and the 1 20.8 
Engineer Battalion. The fifth is occupied by representatives 
of smaller units stationed on base, including a military 
intelligence group and a drone operations unit.The courtyard houses the Estero Bay Military COG 
Memorial , a small columbarium where the cremains of 
personnel who served with distinction are interred.
Like the Academy, the Potentilla is well fortified and sits 
on an elevator system capable of sinking the compound 
beneath the ground in case of an emergency.
#14 Post Exchange (PX)
Security Code 9 : COG MPs, Hardened Medium
Off base, people buy groceries and sundries at bodegas 
and stores like Oasis. On base, we shop at the PX. This 
is literally the only place to buy goods on base, but as a 
result, it is well supplied with not only groceries but also 
other items, including firearms, media, clothing, and con -
sumer electronics. I’m told the PX is the closest thing Night 
City has to a "big box" store, whatever that is.
Shoppers visiting the PX can grab a quick bite at Hot 
Dingo , a SCOPdog and Koff-Pop joint, get their hair cut 
to regulation in the Estero Bay Barber Shop , or do their 
banking at the COG Credit Union . All three are located 
at the front of the building.
#1 5 The Rec Center
Security Code 9 : COG MPs, Hardened Medium
Our one-stop shop for entertainment needs! The Rec 
Center houses a bowling alley on the first floor, an arcade 
and gymnasium on the second floor, and a mini-golf 
course and movie theater on the third.
Just outside is a multi-functional sports field, with 
Biotechnica Techgrass®, capable of reconfiguring its 
marking lines to regulation for multiple game types at the 
touch of a button.

#17 USO Hall
Security Code 7 : COG MPs, Hardened Light

City ManagerCity Manager
A Night City native and political radical with a proven 
track record of community organization, Lucius Rhyne  
(Rockerboy 7 ) is the last person you’d expect to be 
thriving in the muck of city politics. He was elected to the 
post of City Manager last year in a landslide. It’s worth 
noting that the election only happened because of a dis -
trict-wide labor strike that Lucius ignited. The strike forced 
the NCCS to set up the closest thing to free and fair 
elections possible, giving a vote to every SIN-holding 
citizen eighteen and older. Given his popularity at the 
time, Rhyne’s victory was assured. No other candidate 
even came close.
Rhyne’s charismatic and deeply analytical weekly radio 
talks are arguably the most listened-to political content 
in Night City. As City Manager, he’s pushed hard to 
create public-private partnerships to build the community 
resources needed to make the average citizen’s life more 
bearable. During his tenure as City Manager, Rhyne has 
proven surprisingly effective. The residents of the district 
love him, and, so far, the corporations seem willing to 
work with him.
PopulationPopulation
The citizens of the Watson Development are a mix of Night 
City locals, transplants from elsewhere in the Pacifica 
Confederation looking for work, immigrants from Asia 
brought in by the NCCS, and former nomads who aban -
doned the open road when temporary jobs transitioned 
into permanent opportunities. 
The district’s conapts are packed with new families 
enjoying a fragment of stability. Based on admittedly 
incomplete data, Watson has the highest birth rate in the 
city, and the population skews young. That means you’ll 
see a lot of kids playing on the street and a lot of twen -
ty-somethings hitting the clubs. 
Security ProviderSecurity Provider
NCPD’s Precinct #3 provides security for the district. The 
cops here are well-armed, but still smarting from the events 
of last year, when they proved incapable of putting down 
the labor strike. As a result, excessive force complaints are up fifty percent. To say Tom Stone  (Lawman 7 ), the 
precinct’s captain, isn’t a fan of Lucius Rhyne would be an 
understatement. The two almost came to blows during last 
year’s Holographic Holiday Parade.
Recent HistoryRecent History
Obviously, the labor strike and resulting election of Lucius 
Rhyne is the biggest news story to hit the district since the 
4th Corporate War. The strike only lasted three days, but 
the sight of tens of thousands of people standing in the 
street, refusing to move or work, remains one of the most 
impressive moments I’ve ever witnessed. Even the major 
networks couldn’t ignore it. 
The other major news story from the district is construction, 
and everything that goes with it. Megabuildings H10 and 
H1 1 weren’t the first planned by the Night City Council, but 
they were the first to break ground and raise steel thanks to 
heavy petitioning by the NCCS, SovOil, and Petrochem. 
Meanwhile, the northern part of the district is seeing an 
influx of eurobucks as companies build factories there.
The criminal element isn’t staying quiet, either! The Tyger 
Claws, backed by the NCCS, are the dominant underworld 
force in Watson, but they’re facing increasingly stiff competition 
from the slowly rebuilding Kanzaki Yakuza Family. Meanwhile, 
Jimmy "Dequiallo" Miles has reinvented the Arzin Tynon 
gogang, transforming it into a smuggling operation.

Recent HistoryRecent History
Shinobu Nakagawa, the driving force behind the formation 
of Kabuki, passed on in 2042. In the aftermath of his death, 
a bloody dispute, known as the War of Succession, broke 
out over who would inherit his legacy. The War only ended 
after Hayato Nakagawa, Shinobu’s adopted son, slaugh -
tered three rivals in a single evening. Tired of the bloodshed, 
the NCCS stepped in and negotiated a solution: Hayato 
Nakagawa, who renamed himself Shinobu the Second in 
honor of his adoptive father, was confirmed as leader of 
the Tyger Claws. His wife, Ryoko Fujiwara, was installed 
as chairwoman of the Nakagawa Foundation, and Yoshiki 
Murakami was appointed Kabuki’s City Manager.
This did not solve the problem to anyone’s satisfaction. 
While Kabuki, the NCCS, and the Tyger Claws seem 
united to outsiders, tensions roil beneath the surface. 
Ryoko Fujiwara believes in the Nakagawa Foundation's 
mission to uplift the community. She refuses to use the 
money she controls to enhance the wealth of Shinobu the 
Second’s supporters in the Tyger Claws. Gang leaders 
who had hoped for a big payout after supporting the 
current leader are grumbling about either staging a coup 
or defecting to the Kanzaki Family. 
Meanwhile, the NCCS finds Shinobu the Second’s 
leadership skills to be lacking, and many high level Execs 
believe the usefulness of the Tyger Claws is coming to an 
end. The thought is that NCCS member corporations are 
now on firm financial footing, so the days of needing a 
gang to maintain peace and order are gone. 
For now, things in Kabuki are relatively calm, but the 
right spark could start a war.

What’s better than a specialist store selling otaku goods

#8 Kabuki Ceremonial Hall
Security Code 8 : Kimen-Gumi, Hardened Light
To outsiders, our Ceremonial Hall probably seems like 
the strangest location in Kabuki. The outside of this large, 
cylindrical building is designed to resemble the Roman 
Colosseum. Inside, you’ll find a Shinto shrine ( Sankai 
Jinja ), a Buddhist temple ( Sankai-ji ), a Christian chapel 
(Sankai Church ), and a hotel and restaurant ( Sankai-
tei). What the hell, right?
If you’re familiar with Japanese culture, you’ve probably 
already guessed: the Hall is a mall of ceremonial locations, 
where we can hold all of our most important religious and 
cultural events in one place. For example, most women in 
Kabuki aren’t Christian, but they often want a Christian-
style wedding ceremony complete with a beautiful white 
dress. That leads to a demand for a Christian church where 
couples can exchange rings, followed by a second cere -
mony to speak sacred vows before Japanese gods or the 
Buddhas in an appropriate shrine or temple, followed by 
a reception in an upscale restaurant. 
The Ceremonial Hall allows couples to share this beau -
tiful and complex wedding experience without the incon -
venience of long drives between sites. Photographers and videographers are always on hand to document the 
experience for an additional fee. In other words, the Hall 
is a complete wedding experience!
Weddings aren’t the only service offered by the Hall. 
Residents of Kabuki come here for ceremonies honor -
ing everything from birth to death. An on-site nōkanshi 
can ritually wash and prepare bodies for cremation in 
a smoke- and odor-free arc furnace. If needed, profes -
sional mourners are available at reasonable rates. A 
sentō provides sanitation and a traditional shared bathing 
experience.
Tyger Cabs runs a shuttle service to ferry residents back 
and forth between the Ceremonial Hall and Denya Jinja 
in New Westbrook.
#9 Kabuki Market
Security Code 7 : Kimen-Gumi, Hardened Light
The Kabuki Market is an open-air bazaar located in the

Claws Fixer who governs the food stalls in the district. Only 
cooks who truly impress him are granted the privilege of 
opening a stall in Kabuki Market. He also ensures the 
vendors here get first pick of the raw ingredients shipped 
in from Asia by Darangen sea nomads, making the food 
at the Market some of the best in Night City. Honest! This 
place is a scent-and-taste paradise! If you visit, spend 
tons of money and try everything! I’m especially fond of 
the anzu-ame (fruit-flavored paste dipped in syrup and 
covered in candy), isiyaki imo (sweet potatoes roasted 
in hot pebbles), and pon-gashi (made with sweetened 
puffed soybeans instead of the traditional rice or corn). 
Special attention should be paid to Shou-an , a small, 
traditional tea house and the market’s only permanent 
structure.
